# Review pass 2: page-by-page plain-language review

**Method.** All 112 pages, read in order in eight batches of 15 (the last has 7). For each page: a one-line verdict; then the three passages that most hurt a skimming reader, quoted verbatim with a shorter, plainer rewrite that keeps every rule, number, form name, and citation unchanged; then flags for jargon with no plain-language first mention on that page, passive voice that hides who acts (counted by eye), and sentences over 30 words (scripted count from the page's paragraph and list text; table cells excluded). Pages judged fine get one line. Whole pages were not rewritten. The common acronyms IRB, INSPIR, PI, HIPAA, FDA, NIH, BMC and BU were not counted as jargon.

**Totals.** Pages reviewed: 112. Pages marked fine as is: 9 (Exempt_Tip_Sheets_Index, Expedited_Tip_Sheets_Index, Examples_and_Samples, Hub_Consent_HIPAA_Recruitment, Templates_Forms_Library, Hub_Guidance, Hub_About_the_IRB, Staff_Directory, Ask_the_IRB). Sentences over 30 words across the site (scripted): 1,091; the five densest pages are Example_Expedited_Interview_Study (56), Consent_Language_Bank (49), Example_Exempt_Chart_Review (38), Review_Path_Decision_Tool_v3 (37), INSPIR_Task_Checkins_Reports_Closing (25) and Special_Populations (25).

**iRIS:** none found on any page.

Each batch ends with a short "Pass 1 addendum" (heading/content mismatches, within-batch conflicts, visible placeholders) that is summarized in review-pass1.md §5d; the full addenda are kept at the end of this file.
